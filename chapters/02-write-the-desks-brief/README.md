# Chapter 2: the desk's brief

You write the one thing the desk can never write for you: why you own each stock.

[`thesis-cards.txt`](thesis-cards.txt) holds the book's blank thesis card, and under it the candidate card you'll use from Chapter 9. Copy the thesis card once for each of the three stocks you own the most of, and fill it in yourself: "No AI, no searching, just what you believe right now." Save the file as `thesis-cards` and upload it to your Research Desk.

The chapter's other two files, [`memo-template.txt`](../../research-desk/knowledge/memo-template.txt) and [`research-questions.txt`](../../research-desk/knowledge/research-questions.txt), are already in the desk.

## When it's right

> All three of your stocks have a thesis card with a one-sentence reason and two change-my-mind conditions, and each condition is a fact you could find in a filing. You wrote every word before any AI saw it.

Read each change-my-mind line back: could a 10-Q print it? "I'll sell if it feels wrong" can't. "Services grows slower than Products for two quarters" can.
