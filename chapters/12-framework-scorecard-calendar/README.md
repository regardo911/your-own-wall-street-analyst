# Chapter 12: framework, scorecard, calendar

Keep score, so at 30 and 90 days you know whether the desk earns its place.

[`framework.txt`](framework.txt) is your one-page framework. The book describes the page but prints no blank, so this file carries its three headings and closing line. Fill it in your own words; save it to Research Desk as `framework`.

[`scorecard.csv`](scorecard.csv) is Appendix A13's header row. Save it from your spreadsheet app as `scorecard` and load this month: a MEMO row per Week 1 memo, an ALERT row per flagged item in `decisions`, a CANDIDATE row per idea-list name. The values each column takes:

```
Item:          MEMO · ALERT · CANDIDATE
Decision:      BOUGHT · ADDED · HELD · TRIMMED · SOLD · PASSED
Thesis at 90d: HELD / BROKE, with the filing or line that decided it
Candidates:    add "entered buy zone? Y/N, date"
Index:         one broad index fund's closing price on the same dates (optional)
```

Every price is one you look up. "Each return is the later price divided by the starting price, minus 1: $100 at the start and $105 at day 30 is +5%."

[`next-30-days.txt`](next-30-days.txt) goes on your calendar, plus a 30- and a 90-day reminder per scorecard row. [`monthly-review.txt`](monthly-review.txt) is the monthly page.

> Your framework page is written in your own words, the scorecard holds this month's memos, alerts and candidates with a decision and a starting price on every row, and the 30- and 90-day checks are on your calendar with dates.

That's the book's last checkpoint.
