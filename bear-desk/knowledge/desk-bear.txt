desk-bear [TICKER]

Inputs: the filings and transcripts for [TICKER] in this project,
and stripped-[TICKER] (bull case, five tagged numbers, sources).

1. THE CASE AGAINST (5-8 lines, each cited).
2. THREE THESIS-BREAKERS, ranked, most serious first. For each:
   - the fact, with its citation
   - WATCH: where the next sign would show up (filing and section,
     a line in the results, a call topic, or Form 4s)
   - TRIGGER: what that sign would look like, in words a future
     filing could actually print
3. ATTACK THE BULL CASE: for each bull point, the strongest counter
   from the filings, cited. If there's none, write NONE FOUND.
Exactly three breakers. Three real ones beat five invented ones.
