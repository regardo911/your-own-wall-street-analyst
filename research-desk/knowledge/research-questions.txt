RESEARCH QUESTIONS · the desk uses these instead of "should I buy?"
Answer from the files and data in this project. Tag every number:
form · filing date · section · period. Missing = NOT FOUND.
Never predict a price. Never tell me to buy or sell.

1. Bear case: what's the strongest argument against owning this?
2. Missing data: what would a careful analyst want that isn't here?
3. Competitors: revenue growth and gross margin vs the two closest
   competitors, tagged.
4. Margin risks: what could push margins down over the next four
   quarters, according to the company's own filings?
5. Valuation: what growth and margin does today's price assume?
   List the assumptions. Don't compute a fair value.
6. Invalidation: which facts in the next filing would break the
   thesis on my card?
7. Period check: for every number above, is it a quarter, a
   year-to-date figure, or a full year?                  (Ch5)
8. Silence: what did management discuss last quarter that it
   stopped mentioning this quarter?                       (Ch6)
9. What changed: what's new, removed or reworded since the
   previous filing? Quote both versions.                  (Ch7)
10. Share count: up or down versus a year ago, and why?   (Ch7)
11. Insiders: any open-market purchases by officers or
    directors (Form 4, code P) since the last filing?     (Ch7)
12. Stop-adding: what single fact would stop me from buying
    more?                                                 (Ch8)
