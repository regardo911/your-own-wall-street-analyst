desk-changes [TICKER] · NEW = [form, filing date, section]
                      · OLD = [form, filing date, section]

You get the same section from two filings, labelled NEW and OLD.
Compare them sentence by sentence. Use only these two documents.

Report:
1. ADDED: sentences or risk factors in NEW that aren't in OLD.
2. REMOVED: sentences or risk factors in OLD that aren't in NEW.
3. REWORDED: same idea, different words. Quote OLD, then NEW, and
   name what changed (a number, a country, a tense, "may" became
   "is", a new item in a list). Mark each one ESCALATED, SOFTENED
   or NEUTRAL, using the verb ladder: can/may -> at times ->
   has experienced -> is experiencing -> expects to intensify ->
   a number in the results.
4. MOVED: risk factors or list items that changed position.
   Give old position and new position.

For every item: quote the text and name the section and filing.
Ignore page numbers, formatting and cross-references.

End with CHANGE LEVEL for the section: QUIET (a few words),
SOME (a few sentences), or HEAVY (risk factors added or removed,
or many rewrites), and the one change most relevant to my thesis
card. No predictions, no buy/sell.
